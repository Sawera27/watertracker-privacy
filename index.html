Privacy Policy

Water Tracker for Android · Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: Water Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy describes how the Water Tracker app (“the app”), published by Sa'Al (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone. The app does not request internet access and does not communicate with any server operated by us or anyone else.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your drink log: the amount, drink type and time of each entry.
  • Your settings: daily goal, measurement unit (ml or fl oz), reminder on/off, reminder interval, wake-up time and bedtime, and theme.
  • Optionally, the body weight and activity level you type in to receive a suggested daily goal.

This information never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it and cannot recover it for you.

3. Third-party services, advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no social login and no other third-party code that collects data. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
Notifications (Android 13 and later)Only if you turn on reminders. The app shows drink reminders locally on your device. You can decline the permission and still use every other feature.

The app’s background scheduler (Android WorkManager) may also use standard system permissions such as running after a restart so that reminders you chose to enable keep working. These do not give the app access to any personal data.

5. Reminders

Reminders are created and displayed entirely on your device. They are only shown between the wake-up time and bedtime you choose, and they are skipped once you reach your daily goal. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your Android notification settings.

6. Backups

If Android’s backup feature is turned on for your device, the operating system may include the app’s data in your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is a feature of Android that you control in your device settings. We do not have access to these backups.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete everything at any time by opening Settings → Delete all my data in the app, by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage area, which other apps cannot normally read. Because the app does not use the internet, your information is not exposed to network transmission by the app. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and we recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

9. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is a general-audience wellness app and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Health information notice

Water Tracker provides general wellness information only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Suggested goals are rough estimates. Please consult a qualified health professional about your personal hydration needs, especially if you have a medical condition.

11. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete. You remain in full control of the data on your device as described in section 7. If you are located in a region with data protection laws (such as the EEA, UK or California), these rights are satisfied by the controls described in this policy.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the updated version at this web address and update the “Last updated” date above. The in-app privacy policy will be updated in a new app release.
